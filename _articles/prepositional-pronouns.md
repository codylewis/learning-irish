|                            | me[^m]  | you[^ys] | him     | her[^h]  | us[^u]   | you pl.[^yp] | them[^t] |
| -------------------------- | ------- | -------- | ------- | -------- | -------- | ------------ | -------- |
| **ag _(at)_**              | agam    | agat     | aige    | aici     | againn   | agaibh       | acu      |
| **ar _(on, in, at)_**      | orm     | ort      | air     | uirthi   | orainn   | oraibh       | orthu    |
| **as _(from, outof)_**     | asam    | asat     | as      | aisti    | asainn   | asaibh       | astu     |
| **chuig _(toward)_**       | chugam  | chugat   | chuige  | chuici   | chugainn | chugaibh     | chucu    |
| **de _(off, of, from)_**   | dom     | díot     | de      | di       | dínn     | dibh         | díobh    |
| **do _(to, for)_**         | dom     | duit     | dó      | di       | dúinn    | daoibh       | dóibh    |
| **faoi _(about, under)_**  | fúm     | fút      | faoi    | fúithi   | fúinn    | fúibh        | fúthu    |
| **fara _(along with)_**    | umam    | farat    | fare    | fara     | farainn  | faraibh      | fartu    |
| **i _(in)_**               | ionam   | ionat    | ann     | inti     | ionainn  | ionaibh      | iontu    |
| **idir _(between)_**       | idir mé | idir tú  | idir é  | idir í   | eadrainn | eadraibh     | eatarthu |
| **le _(with, by)_**        | liom    | leat     | leis    | léi      | linn     | libh         | leo      |
| **ó _(from)_**             | uaim    | uait     | uaidh   | uaithi   | uainn    | uaibh        | uathu    |
| **roimh _(before)_**       | romham  | romhat   | roimhe  | roimpi   | romhainn | romhaibh     | rompu    |
| **thar _(over)_**          | tharam  | tharat   | thairis | thairist | tharainn | tharaibh     | tharstu  |
| **trí _(through, among)_** | trím    | tríot    | tríd    | tríthi   | trínn    | tríbh        | tríothu  |
| **um*(around)***           | umam    | umat     | uime    | uimpi    | umainn   | umaibh       | umpu     |

[^m]: Almost always ends -m
[^ys]: Almost always ends -t
[^h]: Almost always ends -i
[^u]: Ends -inn or -ínn
[^yp]: Ends in -aibh, -(ao)ibh, or -íbh
[^t]: Almost always ends -u
